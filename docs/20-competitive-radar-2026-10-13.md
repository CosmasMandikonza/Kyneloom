## Executive finding

**High saturation of the shared hackathon brief, not evidence of 126 finished substitutes.** The danger isn't merely a competing visual demo: multiple descriptions explicitly promise verified agentic broadcast commentary, studio approvals, multilingual audiences, interactive pitches, and movement intelligence.

**Do not pitch as novel:** multiple AI agents, live event ingestion, fact-checked match narration, multilingual recaps, broadcast overlays, producer approvals, or 3D replay in isolation. Premier League/Microsoft themselves ask for many of these. ProView3D and other established businesses already market realistic 3D perspectives.

**Best Kyneloom differentiation hypothesis:** *a versioned, evidence-native experience compiler* taking **the same authenticated moment** to (a) immersive, realistically animated interactive 3D, (b) timestamped broadcast overlay output, (c) contextual fan/analyst/accessibility lenses, and (d) a producer-approved, embeddable Playable Moment. A single source/claim/time manifest drives all outputs. It is novel only insofar as our **demonstrated workflow + quality**, not merely a pitch.

## Critical product gap this snapshot exposes

The Microsoft brief requires: ingest → interpret → explain → **render an insight that can appear alongside a match** → personalize, and also mentions automated recap/narrative generation. A beautifully rendered 3D player, **alone**, may fail to demonstrate the organizer's broadcast-overlay use case.

**Required response:** the Experience Compiler produces two synchronized deliverables from the *same* `MomentManifest`:

1. **Living Replay:** realistic, data-timed 3D scene; interactive camera; click runner → evidence; audience lenses.
2. **Broadcast output:** transparent/on-screen overlay layer (HTML/canvas or RGBA pipeline), with `matchClockMs`, scene source refs, proof labels and safe areas. Show it composited over an independently created synthetic match feed or owned/licensed sample video; never import unlicensed Premier League footage. Include an approved recap/caption track.

This adds much more judging and commercial utility than implementing speculative what-if animation. Track as [Issue #31 — broadcast output](https://github.com/CosmasMandikonza/Kyneloom/issues/31).

## One demonstration nobody should confuse with a static dashboard

**15-second story:** cinematic 3D goal pauses; free camera swings behind decoy winger; select run, visually reveal measured defender separation. “The separation increased by X meters” is computed and linked to exact samples; “this may have opened space” labeled hypothesis.

**Next 30 seconds:** switch to *broadcast-compatible on-screen overlay*, showing the **same clock, source fact, and evidence** over a synthetic owned match feed, then change fan/analyst audience lens without changing truth.

**Next 45 seconds:** the Foundry workflow proposes camera/edit/recap tracks; false statistical claim is rejected by deterministic verifier. Producer edits a camera shot, previews and approves a version.

**Final 15 seconds:** open separately authorized published Playable Moment; prove manifest hash, actual trace and the same evidence. Demo needs a strong live working path; no fabricated “verification passed” animation.

All interaction must be **real and responsive**. If time is tight, make one sequence exceptional; defer extras such as gallery particle effects before cutting overlay compliance, fact-grounding or Studio editing.
